# Memories and story videos

**Memories** turns indexed local photos and clips into reviewable story suggestions and exported videos. Creating suggestions is user-triggered; opening the page does not automatically export a movie.

## Find or suggest a story

- Choose **Refresh** to reload the cards already available.
- Choose **Generate memories** or **Find new memories** to search the indexed archive for additional story candidates.
- To steer the search, enter a topic or phrase and submit it. For example, use an event, place, activity or person that is already represented in your indexed library.
- Suggestions depend on what has been indexed and on available dates, people, locations and quality metadata. No suggestion is a guarantee that every relevant file was found.

## Review and export

1. Open a suggestion and review its photos and clip choices.
2. Adjust the photo count, duration, mood/pacing or soundtrack controls that are available in the editor.
3. Preview the result and check the selected music and original audio level.
4. Choose **Choose location & export** and confirm the destination.
5. Watch the export progress. Cancel if needed; the original media remains unchanged.
6. Reopen or play the generated movie from the Memories screen or the chosen destination.

The soundtrack is selected locally from available music. A missing/offline track may cause the story's own audio or another fallback to be used, depending on the story and build.

Removing a suggestion card or clearing suggestions removes the card from Silo's list; it does not delete the source photos. The generated movie is a separate output file. Settings lets you choose where movies and story-support files are saved, or return to Silo's app storage.

## Limits

Memory discovery depends on completed indexes and the content present in the source library. It runs only when requested, but generating a story and rendering a video can use substantial CPU and disk space. Check the destination has enough free storage. Silo does not upload local media to an online AI service for this workflow.
