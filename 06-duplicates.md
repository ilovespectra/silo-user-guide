# Duplicates

The Duplicates page is designed for careful cleanup. Silo compares exact file contents; it is not a “similar-looking photo” finder.

## Scan and review

1. Open **Duplicates** and choose the scan action.
2. Let the scan finish for the sources you want compared. Unavailable sources cannot contribute files.
3. Open a group and compare the file details and previews.
4. Keep the protected/original item in each group. Select only copies you have checked.
5. Stage selected copies in the **Review removed** area. This is the recoverable review step, not final deletion.
6. Open the review area and verify the list. Restore any item you want to keep.
7. Permanently delete only after checking the exact selection and confirming you have another copy if needed.

## Recovery and safety

Items staged for removal remain recoverable until permanent deletion. Use **Restore** to return selected items. **Delete selected permanently** and **Clear all permanently** cannot be undone. Never rely on a thumbnail or filename alone; compare source, path, size and preview, and verify your backup before permanently deleting anything.

## What counts as a duplicate

The scan looks for files with matching content, not just matching names. It will not mark two resized, edited, recompressed or visually similar images as exact duplicates. Conversely, byte-identical files can be duplicates even if they live in different folders.

If no groups appear, check that the Duplicate index is complete, the sources are mounted and readable, and the scan includes the intended source scope.
